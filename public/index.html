GOT QUESTIONS?

Good to know.

Why are you collecting donations?＋

Donations help Teddy Tech Inc maintain VPS servers, hosting infrastructure, domains, and technology projects.

Are donations compulsory?＋

No. All donations are voluntary.

Can I choose my own donation amount?＋

Yes. You can enter any amount accepted by the configured payment provider.

Can I donate using a card?＋

Yes, where card payments are enabled for the configured Paystack account.

Can I donate using mobile money?＋

Mobile money is available only where supported and enabled for the configured Paystack account and currency.

Will I receive a payment confirmation?＋

Once Paystack confirms your payment, the website will display the appropriate confirmation.

Is my payment information secure?＋

Payments are processed through Paystack. The website verifies transactions on the server and does not store full card numbers or CVVs.

Can I donate anonymously?＋

Yes. You may choose to hide your name from public displays. Payment providers may still process information required for payment and compliance.
